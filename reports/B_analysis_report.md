# Deliverable B — Data Analysis Report

## B1.1 Revenue per hectare by crop
Teff earns the highest revenue per hectare (~148k Birr/ha) despite lower physical yield, because of its premium price. Maize has the highest physical yield but ranks lower on revenue.

## B1.2 Price trend 2021–2024
All crops rose in price. The relative ranking and size of the gaps matter for profitability decisions.

## B2.1 Yield by region
Highest average yields: Tigray / Amhara / Oromia.  
Lowest and most variable: Somali (harsh agro-climatic conditions).

## B2.2 Yield by crop type
Maize highest (~3.9 t/ha), teff lowest (~2.0 t/ha). Ranking matches known agronomic potential.

## B2.3 Region × Crop pivot
Best combination: Maize in SNNPR / Oromia.  
Worst: Teff / sorghum in Somali.  
Pattern reflects rainfall and altitude suitability.

## B2.4 Improved seed gap by crop
Positive yield lift for every crop. Magnitude varies; some crops benefit more in both absolute and percentage terms.

## B2.5 Pest/disease gap by crop
Pest/disease pressure reduces yield for all crops. Some crops are hit harder in absolute and relative terms.

## B3.1 Fertilizer response
Positive response with diminishing returns (flattening at high rates) for most crops – classic agronomic pattern.

## B3.2 Altitude by crop
Each crop has a preferred altitude band. Cells with <30 plots are low-confidence.

## B3.3 Planting month
Timing matters. Effect size in t/ha should be compared with region and crop effects.

## B3.4 Distance to market
Weak negative correlation with yield. Keep as a weak feature, not a primary driver.

## B4.1 Yield trend by year
Year-to-year changes are small relative to within-year spread (~1.2 t/ha). No strong secular trend.

## B4.2 Weather anomalies
Most unusual region-year identified via temperature deviation from the region’s own 4-year mean. Yield impact checked by crop.

## B4.3 Plot-reported vs station rainfall
Moderate correlation. Differences expected because plot reports are local/subjective while station data are regional averages. Both carry signal.
